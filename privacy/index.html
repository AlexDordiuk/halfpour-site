Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

In short: Halfpour has no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. What you log stays on your iPhone. Apple Health is optional, and Health data never leaves your device through Halfpour.

Halfpour is an app that helps you drink less, take an alcohol-free month or quit. It is made by Oleksandr Dordiuk, an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Data stored on your device

When you use Halfpour, the app stores the following on your iPhone:

  • your drinks: time, name, volume, strength, grams of alcohol, portion, price and optional notes;
  • days you mark as alcohol-free;
  • your plan: goal, country standard drink, weekly line, start dates and the time your day starts;
  • cravings you record: time, strength, trigger and outcome;
  • your dreams (what your savings go to) and their prices;
  • Apple Pay spends, if you set them up: amount, currency and the place's name;
  • settings such as drink prices, currency, notification and privacy preferences.

This data is shared only with Halfpour's own widgets and, if you use one, Halfpour on your Apple Watch. The phone and the watch exchange it directly through Apple's WatchConnectivity. It is never sent to us. Halfpour has no iCloud sync: your log is kept only on this iPhone, and your backup is Apple Health and the CSV export.

Apple Health (optional)

If you allow it, Halfpour saves each drink to Apple Health as "Number of Alcoholic Beverages", counted in your country's standard drinks, with the volume, strength, grams of alcohol and drink name attached. Halfpour reads back only the drinks it saved itself, to restore your log after a reinstall.

The optional Morning after screen (Halfpour Pro) reads your sleep, resting heart rate and heart rate variability from Apple Health, only when you open it and only after you allow it. The comparison is calculated on your iPhone. Halfpour does not store this Health data, does not put it in iCloud and never sends it to us or anyone else. Health data is not used for advertising or any other purpose.

You can turn off access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Halfpour, and delete the drinks Halfpour saved there.

Apple Pay spends (optional)

If you set up a Shortcuts automation, it passes the amount and the place's name of a payment to Halfpour. Halfpour stores them on your iPhone to count the money you spent on drinks. Halfpour has no access to Wallet, your cards or your bank, and only sees what your automation passes to it.

Purchases

Purchases of Halfpour Pro are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To check that a purchase is valid and to unlock Pro, the app may use RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, information about your purchases and transactions, and basic technical data such as device model, OS version, app version and country. It does not receive your name, email or anything you log. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

App Store statistics

If you allowed sharing with app developers in iOS (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple shares aggregated App Store statistics and crash reports with us. Apple decides what is included; see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications and Face ID

Reminders, milestones and the weekly summary are scheduled on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us. If you turn on the lock, Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode is checked by iOS; Halfpour only learns whether it succeeded.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking, and the app does not ask for permission to track you.
  • We don't use analytics or crash reporting services: the app sends no usage statistics.

Health information

What you log about alcohol is personal, which is why it stays on your iPhone. Halfpour is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. If stopping drinking makes you unwell, talk to a doctor.

Deleting your data

You can delete any drink, spend or dream in the app. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your iPhone. Drinks saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Age

Halfpour is intended for adults (18+).

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics, ads or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
