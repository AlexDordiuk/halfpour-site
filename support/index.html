Support

Halfpour helps you drink less, take an alcohol-free month or quit, at your own pace.

Write to drawen77@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days. If something isn't working, please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I log a drink without opening the app?

Add the Halfpour widget to your Home Screen, or the Log a drink button to Control Center or the Lock Screen (iOS 18 or later). You can also say "Log a drink in Halfpour" to Siri. With Halfpour Pro, you can log from your Apple Watch.

How do I mark a day as alcohol-free?

Tap Alcohol-free today on This week, in the medium widget, on Apple Watch or with the Control Center button, or say "Mark today alcohol-free in Halfpour". Days you don't mark stay blank, so your streaks stay honest.

What is a standard drink here?

Halfpour counts in your country's standard: a US drink is 14 g of alcohol, a UK unit 8 g, an Australian standard drink 10 g, a Canadian drink 13.45 g, a Nordic one 12 g. You can change it in Settings › Plan. Every drink keeps its grams, so changing the standard recounts your history without losing anything.

What is free and what is Pro?

Logging, your weekly line and plan, alcohol-free days, the alcohol-free month and Quit with milestones, editing any day, CSV export, Apple Health, money saved with three dreams, Apple Pay spends, the small widget, Lock Screen widgets, Control Center, Siri, the breathing exercise and the neutral icons are free. Halfpour Pro adds full history and insights, Morning after, Apple Watch, the medium widget, more exercises and dreams. Pro is a yearly subscription with a free week, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Halfpour › Settings › Manage subscription, or on your iPhone open Settings › your name › Subscriptions, choose Halfpour and tap Cancel Subscription. Halfpour reminds you 2 days before a free trial ends. See also Apple's guide.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Halfpour › Settings › Halfpour Pro and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I get my log back after a reinstall?

If you turned on Save drinks to Apple Health, Halfpour offers to restore your drinks when your log is empty. You can also tap Settings › Apple Health › Restore from Apple Health. Alcohol-free day marks and prices aren't stored in Health; export a CSV now and then if you want a full copy.

How do Apple Pay spends work?

A personal automation in Shortcuts (Transaction) runs the Add alcohol spend action after you pay with a Wallet card at a place you chose. Halfpour then asks how many drinks it was. Settings › Apple Pay spends has the steps and a Test button. The trigger only works for Wallet cards, can time out or fire on a declined payment, so every spend can be fixed or deleted.

My Apple Watch shows a different number

Open Halfpour on your iPhone once and keep the watch nearby; the watch updates from the phone. If the problem stays, write to us.

Medical note

Halfpour is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. If you drink every day or feel shaky, sweaty or anxious without alcohol, stopping suddenly can be dangerous: talk to a doctor first. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
