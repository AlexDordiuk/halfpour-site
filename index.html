Drink less, take a month off, or quit

Halfpour counts your drinks in your country's standard drinks, shows your week against a line that steps down until your goal, and keeps the money you don't spend.

  • Any volume and strength: pints, schooners, 33 cl, 175 ml, your own.
  • Alcohol-free days in one tap, honest streaks, an alcohol-free month or Quit with milestones.
  • Apple Health, Apple Watch, widgets, Control Center and Siri.
  • No account. Your log stays on your iPhone. The basics are free.
